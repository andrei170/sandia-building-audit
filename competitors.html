What this table does and does not tell you

It tells you the standard in Albuquerque roofing is competent. All three readable firms have the basics — description, canonical, schema, analytics, click-to-call. Nobody here is leaving obvious gaps.

It does not tell you where you sit, because we could not read your site. Two of the five firms in this market sit behind the same SiteGround challenge, yours included. That is a coincidence of hosting, not a finding about either of you.

Finding 1 — Premium Roofing Is The Firm To Watch
Premium Roofing 1,760 words · 197KB · 7 H2 premiumroofingnm.com 43 years, family owned and operated GA4 + Google Tag Manager + Google Ads conversion tag + Meta pixel schema · canonical · description · click-to-call
The complete set, and it compounds

Analytics, tag manager, conversion tracking and a Meta pixel. That combination means they can see which ad produced which enquiry, cut the ones that fail, and retarget people who did not convert the first time.

Every month that runs, their cost per job falls and a competitor without tracking stays flat. That is the real threat — not their word count, not their 43 years, but a feedback loop you do not currently have.

They are family owned, not corporate. So this is not a budget you cannot match. It is a setup you can copy in an afternoon.

Finding 2 — AAA Has The Content Lead And Two H1s
AAA Roofing 1,828 words · 8 H2 · 2 H1 tags aaaroofingnm.com 30+ years · residential and commercial GA4 · schema · canonical · click-to-call · no ads tracking
The highest word count in the market, and a fixable flaw

AAA publishes the most content of the three and has the same 30-year claim you do. They are the direct like-for-like comparison: same city, same age, same residential-and-commercial mix.

They have two H1 tags, which muddles what the page is about, and no conversion tracking. So the firm with the most content in Albuquerque still cannot tell which of those 1,828 words produces a phone call.

Finding 3 — Alvarado Shows The Floor Is Lower Than You Think
Alvarado Roofing 820 words · 6 H2 · A+ BBB alvaradoroofing.com GA4 · schema · canonical · click-to-call no tag manager, no ads tracking
820 words and a complete set of basics is enough to compete here

Less than half AAA's content, and they hold a position in this market. The lesson is the same one that shows up in every market we measure: the basics done completely beat a lot of words done carelessly.

For you that sets a realistic bar. If your pages are near 800 words with description, canonical, schema, analytics and a phone link, you are structurally competitive in Albuquerque roofing today.

Finding 4 — Nobody Here Is Competing On Exclusivity
WHAT ALL THREE COMPETITORS SELL ON Premium Roofing 43 years, family owned, financing AAA Roofing 30+ years, experience, local professionals Alvarado Roofing A+ BBB rating, contractor credentials NONE of them is saying: "call us directly and you are not bidding against three other contractors."
This is the position that answers your actual complaint

Every firm in this market sells the same things: years, credentials, family ownership. Those are table stakes in Albuquerque and they do not separate anyone.

What separates you is what you told Jocelyne you want — a homeowner who contacts you directly gets one contractor, one visit, one honest price, and is not being called by three other firms the same afternoon. That is a genuine benefit to the customer, not just to you.

It is also self-reinforcing. Every direct enquiry is one you do not pay a platform for, at a price you did not have to cut to win.

What This Field Tells Us To Do
  • Copy Premium Roofing's measurement setup first. GA4, Tag Manager, conversion tracking. It is the one thing a competitor here is doing that genuinely compounds, and it is cheap.
  • Target Alvarado's 820 words, not AAA's 1,828. The basics done completely is the bar in this market.
  • Extend the location pages across the metro — you already have Albuquerque and Los Lunas, so the pattern exists. Rio Rancho, Bernalillo, Belen, Corrales next.
  • Lead on exclusivity, because nobody else does. One contractor, one visit, no bidding war — it is the only claim in this market that is about the customer's experience rather than the company's age.
  • Then paid, tracked and fenced to the metro, so a growing share of your work arrives without an auction attached.

Three competitors selling years and credentials. Nobody selling “you are not bidding against anyone”.

That is the gap, and it is the same thing you said you were tired of.
