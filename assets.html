The Short Version
1. A SHARED LEAD IS SOLD TO YOUR COMPETITORS TOO One homeowner enquiry -> sold to you AND to three others. You pay either way. You win it by calling fastest or quoting lowest. Both cost you margin on every job. That is the platform working as designed, not failing. 2. ONE ALBUQUERQUE COMPETITOR IS MEASURING PAID ADS Premium Roofing runs GA4 + Tag Manager + a Google Ads conversion tag + a Meta pixel. They are the ONLY firm we have found doing that across four markets this week. Their cost per job falls every month. Yours cannot. They are 43 years old and family owned -- not outspending you. 3. YOUR STRUCTURE IS ALREADY RIGHT Separate service pages: roofing, roof-repairs, remodeling, flooring. Separate location pages: albuquerque-nm, los-lunas. Most contractors we audit do not have this. The pattern works -- it just stops after two towns. 4. NOBODY IN THIS MARKET SELLS EXCLUSIVITY All three competitors sell years, credentials and family ownership. Not one says "call us and you are not bidding against three other contractors." That is the one claim that is about the customer, and it is the exact thing you told Jocelyne you were tired of.
Why This Answers What You Actually Asked
Two to four jobs a week does not need twice the leads

It needs a share of your enquiries to arrive uncontested. A direct enquiry closes at a higher rate, at a better price, and costs you nothing per lead. Ten of those are worth more than thirty shared ones.

And it compounds in the right direction. Every location page that ranks, and every tracked ad that works, keeps producing after it is built. A platform subscription produces nothing the month you stop paying.

We are not suggesting you switch anything off on day one. Angi keeps the lights on while an owned channel is built underneath it. The goal is to change the mix, not to gamble the pipeline.

What We Would Establish On The Call
  • What a platform job actually costs you — fees paid per job won, not per lead. That one number decides whether any of this is worth doing.
  • What is already on your site. We could not read it. Analytics, conversion tracking and schema take five minutes to check together.
  • Which of the four services you want more of. Roofing, remodeling, flooring and restoration are four different buyers, and chasing all four equally is how a budget disappears.
  • Then: measurement first, the rest of the metro's location pages, and tracked ads — so a growing share of the work arrives with no auction attached.

Thirty years, eight thousand roofs, and the right site structure already.
