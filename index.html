You Are Not Short Of Leads. You Are Renting Them, And You Are Buying Them Twice.

You said you are doing one to two jobs a week, you want two to four, and you are tired of competing for the same lead on Angi. That is a demand-ownership problem, not a website problem.

On a shared-lead platform the same enquiry is sold to several contractors at once. So every job is won on who calls back first and who drops their price, and you pay for the lead whether you win it or not. The harder you work the platform, the more it costs to stand still.

Going from one or two jobs a week to three or four does not require more shared leads. It requires a channel where the enquiry arrives addressed to you and nobody else is quoting it.

30 years • 8,000+ roofs • Real site structure • Demand rented, not owned

Finding 1 — A Shared Lead Is Sold To Your Competitors At The Same Moment It Is Sold To You
HOW A SHARED-LEAD PLATFORM WORKS Homeowner submits one enquiry | +---> sold to you +---> sold to competitor A +---> sold to competitor B +---> sold to competitor C YOU PAY FOR IT EITHER WAY. You win it by calling first or quoting lowest. Both of those cost you margin. HOW AN OWNED CHANNEL WORKS Homeowner searches, finds YOUR page, calls YOU | +---> nobody else is quoting it
This is the actual answer to what you asked for

You told Jocelyne you do not like competing for leads. That is not a complaint about the platform's quality — it is the platform working exactly as designed. Shared leads are the product.

The way out is not a better lead source. It is owning the place the enquiry starts. Your own pages ranking for “roof repair Albuquerque”, your Google profile, and tracked advertising that sends people to you rather than into an auction.

Two to four jobs a week does not need twice the leads. It needs a share of your enquiries to arrive uncontested, because those close at a higher rate and at a better price.

This is the one competitive fact worth acting on

Premium Roofing is running a Google Ads conversion tag. Across every contractor and installer website we have measured this week, in four separate markets, they are the only firm we have found doing it.

That means they can see which ads produce jobs and which waste money. Over time that compounds: they cut what fails and double what works, while a competitor without tracking keeps paying for both.

They are 43 years old and family owned, so they are not outspending you with venture money. They are just measuring. That is the gap worth closing, and it is cheap to close.

Finding 3 — Your Structure Is Already Right, Which Is Rare

You have the thing most contractors are missing

Separate service pages and separate location pages, each with its own title. That is the structure that lets a page rank for “roof repair Albuquerque” rather than having one page try to rank for everything. Most firms we audit do not have this.

Los Lunas already has its own page, which means somebody has thought about geography properly. The obvious next step is the rest of the metro — Rio Rancho, Bernalillo, Belen, Corrales — on the same pattern.

So this is not a rebuild conversation. You have 30 years, 8,000 roofs, and a sensibly built site. The question is where the enquiries come from, not whether the website works.

What We Would Establish On The Call
  • What an Angi job actually costs you — lead fees paid per job won, not per lead. That single number decides everything else.
  • What is already installed on the site. We could not read it. Analytics, conversion tracking and schema are five minutes to check together.
  • Whether the bot wall is turned up too high. Google is getting through, which is what matters most, but it is worth confirming nothing else useful is being blocked.
  • Which of the four services you actually want more of. Roofing, remodeling, flooring and restoration are four different buyers, and chasing all four equally is how a marketing budget disappears.
  • Then: tracking, the rest of the metro's location pages, and tracked ads — so a growing share of your work arrives without an auction attached.

One to two jobs a week from leads you rent. Three to four needs leads you own.

You already have 30 years, 8,000 roofs and the right site structure. What is missing is the channel.
